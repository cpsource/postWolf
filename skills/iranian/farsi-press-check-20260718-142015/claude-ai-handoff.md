## Terminology gap check

Machine translation collapses Farsi distinctions that are often the actual story (e.g. تفاهم‌نامه = non-binding MOU vs توافق = agreement). Treat any gap below as a **finding**, not a translation artifact.

**Loaded terms found across outlets:**

- **تجاوز** (tajāvoz) — "aggression / assault". Casts the other side as the aggressor (vs neutral حمله 'attack'). Editorial. _[Javan, Mashregh]_
- **تفاهم‌نامه** (tafāhom-nāmeh) — "memorandum of understanding (MOU)". Non-binding. Deliberately weaker than a treaty — signals nothing binding was conceded. _[Jamaran]_
- **تفاهم** (tafāhom) — "understanding". Mutual understanding. Non-binding; no obligations created. _[Tasnim]_
- **توقف** (tavaqof) — "halt / stoppage". A halt — can be unilateral, temporary, conditional. Weaker than a ceasefire. _[Tasnim]_
