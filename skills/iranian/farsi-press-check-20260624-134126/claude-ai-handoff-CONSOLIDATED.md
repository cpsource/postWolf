## THE STATE OF PLAY (as the Iranian press frames it)

The negotiations are over implementation of the **"Islamabad Declaration" / تفاهم‌نامه اسلام‌آباد** — explicitly a *tafāhom-nāmeh* (non-binding **memorandum of understanding**), NOT a *tavāfoq* (binding agreement). An Iranian delegation ("Minab 168," incl. Qalibaf and Araghchi) held ~18 hours of talks in Switzerland (Zurich/Geneva), including an 80-minute quadrilateral session with US, Qatar, and Pakistan, returning to Tehran Monday 1 Tir / ~June 22. The fight is over **Clause 13** sequencing: Iran demands the US first execute end-of-war on all fronts (esp. Lebanon), lift the naval blockade, reopen the Strait of Hormuz, issue oil-sanction waivers, and unfreeze blocked assets — *before* moving to final-agreement talks. Iran says if Clause 13 (priority: Lebanon) isn't implemented, **its commitments are reversible** (بازگشت‌پذیر).

## TERMINOLOGY GAP CHECK (read this — it IS the finding)

The single most important pattern across **every faction**: what Western reporting may call a **"deal" or "ceasefire agreement," the Iranian press uniformly calls a تفاهم‌نامه (tafāhom-nāmeh) — a non-binding memorandum of understanding.** This is not incidental; it's load-bearing:

- **تفاهم‌نامه (MOU) vs توافق (agreement):** Even the **Russian Kremlin** is quoted making the exact distinction — "no *agreement* yet, only a *memorandum*, a document at a different level." Iran reserves توافق ("final agreement") for a *future* stage gated behind US implementation of Clause 13. → A Western "they reached a deal" claim is **overstated** by Iranian lights.
- **تعلیق تحریم (suspension) vs لغو (lifting):** Kayhan speaks of sanctions *suspension* — temporary, reversible — not *lifting*. Watch any Western "sanctions lifted" claim against this.
- **آتش‌بس (true ceasefire) vs توقف (halt):** Both appear. Kayhan uses آتش‌بس for the April Lebanon ceasefire but frames the current state as a fragile, **reversible (بازگشت‌پذیر)** halt that Iran will undo if the US reneges.
- **فضاسازی / جوسازی (spin / manufactured atmosphere):** Both Kayhan (re Trump/Hormuz) and Mashregh (re Grossi/IAEA) deploy these pejoratives — a tell that they're dismissing Western/US claims as propaganda, not reporting them as fact.
