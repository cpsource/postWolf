## The Western claim (for reference)

- **Trump (Truth Social, Mon night):** US representatives will "meet with Iran tomorrow" (Tue June 30) in Qatar; envoys **Witkoff and Kushner** travel to Doha for high-level meetings on the Iran file. (CNBC, CNN, Axios)
- Framing: a **new round of US–Iran talks/negotiations in Doha** over the Strait of Hormuz, following the weekend strikes and a mutual halt to attacks.

## What the Iranian side actually says

The Iranian government does **not** deny sending a delegation to Doha — but flatly rejects the characterization that it is **meeting/negotiating with the US** there.

**Esmail Baghaei (Foreign Ministry / negotiating-team spokesman):**
- «طی روزهای آتی هیچ نشست مذاکراتی در هیچ سطحی با طرف آمریکایی نداریم»
  — *"In the coming days we have no negotiating meeting at any level with the American side."*
- «اینکه نمایندگان آمریکا به قطر سفر می‌کنند، ارتباطی با سفر هیئت ایرانی ندارد»
  — *"The US representatives traveling to Qatar has no connection to the Iranian delegation's trip."*
- The Iranian expert delegation goes to Doha **solely to follow up implementation of the MOU (تفاهم‌نامه)** — specifically unfreezing Iran's blocked assets (cited as clause 11) — **not** to hold direct talks with Americans.

**Kazem Gharibabadi (Deputy Foreign Minister):**
- «اخبار منتشرشده درباره برگزاری مذاکرات فنی در دوحه صحت ندارد»
  — *"Reports of technical [working-group] talks being held in Doha are not accurate."* Technical talks would come only after preconditions are met.

**Tabnak headline (conservative) captures the split precisely:**
«توافق ایران و آمریکا بر سر توقف حملات / دور جدید مذاکرات در دوحه **تکذیب شد** / ترامپ: فردا نمایندگان این کشور با ایران دیدار می‌کنند»
— *"Iran–US agreement on halting attacks / a new round of negotiations in Doha is **denied** / Trump: tomorrow our country's representatives will meet with Iran."* The Iranian outlet literally prints the denial and Trump's claim side by side.

## Loaded terminology detected (the actual finding)

| Western word | Farsi word used by Iran | What the gap means |
|---|---|---|
| "talks / negotiations / meeting **with** Iran" | **پیگیری اجرا** (peygiri-ye ejra) — *follow-up on implementation*; explicitly **نه مذاکره** (not negotiation) | Iran frames Doha as administering an existing deal, not opening a new one. The US frames it as negotiations. |
| "the deal / agreement" | **تفاهم‌نامه** (tafāhom-nāmeh) — *non-binding MOU* | Iran insists what exists is an MOU whose clauses must be *implemented first*; final-agreement talks are explicitly **conditioned** on that. |
| "direct US–Iran meeting" | mediated via **Qatar (and Pakistan)** — هیئت ایرانی + میانجی قطری | Iran presents Qatar as mediator carrying messages, not a venue where the two sides sit together. Hence "no connection between the two trips." |
| "ceasefire" | **توقف حملات** (tavaqof-e hamalāt) — *halt of attacks* | A halt, framed as mutual/temporary, not a formal ceasefire. |

**Bottom-line gap:** Both sides agree Iranians and Americans will be **in Doha this week**. They disagree on the verb. Washington says *negotiating with Iran*; Tehran says *following up MOU implementation, with Qatar mediating — and no direct negotiating session at any level*. That verb is the whole story.

## Notes for Claude.ai analysis

- The denial is **partly real, partly face-saving.** US and Iranian principals being in the same city, with Qatari shuttle mediation and a deputy-FM-level Iranian team, is functionally proximity talks even if Tehran refuses the word "negotiation." Weigh the literal denial against the operational reality.
- Watch the **precondition logic**: Iran says final-deal talks start only *after* MOU clauses (assets, oil sales) are implemented. So "we're only following up implementation" is also a negotiating posture — sequencing leverage, not just semantics.
- **Domestic driver:** Kayhan + the 2,000-clerics letter show why Pezeshkian's government can't call this "negotiations" at home. The denial serves the internal audience as much as it describes the facts.
- **Missing/contested:** No Iranian source confirms a *bilateral negotiating session*. Tasnim (IRGC wire) unreachable this run — worth a re-fetch for the security-faction read.
